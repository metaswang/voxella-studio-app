VoxStudio Lifetime
Product ID: com.voxella.studio.lifetime
Type: Non-consumable, one-time purchase. No subscription or automatic renewal.

What this purchase provides
Lifetime access to VoxStudio on Mac, including unlimited new projects and free software upgrades for life. Existing projects remain available. Cloud AI credits and Starter/Pro subscriptions are separate and are not included in this purchase.

How to locate and test the purchase
1. Launch the Mac app. A VoxStudio account is not required to purchase or restore Lifetime access.
2. Click Settings (the gear button in the app sidebar), then select Account. The Lifetime access card is shown at the top of the Account screen. The main window may also show a Buy Lifetime shortcut when this Mac does not have Lifetime access.
3. On an Apple Account that does not already own this product, the card shows the localized App Store price and a Buy Lifetime button. Click Buy Lifetime to open Apple's StoreKit purchase sheet.
4. Complete the transaction using the App Review purchase environment. After StoreKit verifies the transaction, the card changes to Purchased / Lifetime purchased, and new project creation is unlocked.
5. To restore a previous purchase, return to Settings > Account and click Restore purchases using the same Apple Account.
